# Build 61

Treaty judgments acquired remedies. Build 60 deliberately separated legal validity from operational finality: a stabilization-board action could be declared ultra vires after reserves had already moved or domestic conditions had already tightened, and Article VII withdrawal could end institutional membership without erasing external debt. Build 61 accepts that the past still cannot be rolled back. Instead, it creates a persistent Interreality Recognition and Remedies Office that turns some surviving legal claims into compensation awards, recognition proceedings, execution records, and liens on later liquidity.

An ultra-vires Build 60 treaty case can now produce a restitution award. The award is linked to the treaty case and underlying Build 59 motion, but it does not change either object's historical result. Recognition treats the tribunal's ultra-vires judgment as final for remedial purposes, and execution pays from the stabilization fund's matching reserve pool into the claimant reality's foreign reserves when liquidity is available. The simulation can therefore contain both `unlawful reserve decision that cannot be unspent` and a later compensation payment for that same unlawful act. The remedy changes the balance sheet now; it does not repair causality then.

Completed Article VII withdrawals can also become post-membership debt awards when Build 57 external debt remains outstanding. The creditor reality may register the surviving obligation even though the debtor reality is no longer a treaty member. Recognition rests on Article VII's explicit survival of accrued obligations. Existing sovereign reserve stock is protected by default, so recognition alone does not immediately seize the reserves a withdrawing member already has. Instead, the recognized award activates a persistent lien on later foreign-reserve inflows. When the debtor reality later gains reserves, thirty-five percent of the new inflow is automatically garnished toward the recognized debt until the award is satisfied. The old debt can therefore attach itself to money that did not exist when the treaty relationship ended.

A debtor can waive reserve immunity manually, permitting immediate attachment of available foreign reserves. Otherwise, the distinction between protected reserve stock and attachable future inflows remains visible in the award. This creates a new split between formal sovereignty and economic execution: membership can end, current reserves can remain immune, and yet future liquidity can still arrive encumbered. Build 61 records recognition separately from execution so a legal entitlement, an enforceable award, and actual payment remain different states.

Build 61 continues the project's layered persistence model through `nothing-state-v61` and preserves the existing save/render wrapper chain. It does not replace or rewrite Build 60 treaty cases, Build 59 fund motions, Build 58 stabilization programs, Build 57 external debt, or Build 56 foreign-reserve balances. It only reads those layers and adds remedial consequences above them. The final script remains loaded after `treaty.js`, and the forget action now clears state through version 61.

Build 61 emits remedy-office creation, restitution awards, debt awards, recognition decisions, fund restitution, immunity waivers, future-inflow garnishments, satisfaction events, and remedy crises into the existing trust-audit, memory, place, and observability systems. New places can include `invalid decision survived beside a valid compensation order`, `left the institution but the creditor kept an address`, `treaty exit with a court order attached`, `judgment paid without reversing the decision`, `law became a lien on money that did not exist yet`, and `future reserve inflow arrived already owing the past`. The bailiff can now find itself serving a judgment on assets that arrived after the case ended.

The Build 61 harness covers both core paths. An ultra-vires reserve-tranche case produced a recognized 3.0-unit restitution award, reduced the matching stabilization reserve pool by 3.0, increased the claimant's foreign reserves by 3.0, and reached satisfied status without modifying the old motion. A completed withdrawal with 12.0 of surviving external debt produced a recognized post-membership award whose current reserve stock remained protected; after a simulated 10.0-unit foreign-reserve inflow, the active lien garnished 3.5 units, reduced external debt to 8.5, and preserved the remaining 6.5 units of the new inflow. Syntax validation for `remedies.js` also passes under Node.
