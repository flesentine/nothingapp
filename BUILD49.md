# Build 49

Procurement acquired oversight. Reality A and Reality B now each maintain a persistent Office of Package Procurement Integrity with independence, credibility, backlog, findings, protest history, and capture state. Awarded Build 48 procurements can be audited for criteria capture, sole-source abuse, serial no-bid renewals, vendor lock-in, unresolved performance breaches, sponsor affiliation, and debarred vendors that remain attached to an award. Oversight findings are also emitted into the existing Build 40 trust-audit stream, allowing Build 41 transparency, Build 42 finality, and Build 43 execution to eventually treat compliance conclusions as part of institutional history.

Losing bidders can file persistent bid protests. A protest can be denied or sustained by the inspector. Sustaining a protest stays the Build 48 contract and marks the procurement protested, but it does not uninstall Build 44 artifacts or erase Build 45 SBOMs that were already deployed under the award. This creates a recurring split between legal procurement authority and operational software reality: the contract may be stayed while the code continues running. Sustained protests can also open ordinary observability incidents, allowing the legal defect itself to enter the pager/runbook system.

Compliance findings can generate corrective-action orders to stay a contract, rescind an award, or debar a vendor. Rescission removes future procurement authority and can dispute the vendor's Build 46 contractor-maintainer seat without reversing releases already published or binaries already executed. Debarment makes the vendor ineligible for future Build 48 competition while its existing code can remain in production, producing the deliberately bureaucratic state `debarred vendor still inside production SBOM`.

Vendors can submit self-attestations claiming that corrective actions are complete. Accepting self-attestation can lift a contract stay and close a finding without independent verification, but repeated reliance reduces inspector independence and credibility. Enough self-certified compliance can capture the oversight office itself, turning the regulated vendor into the source of evidence used by the watchdog. An independent verification path can instead restore some inspector credibility. A captured inspector can later be recused, but prior audit findings, denials, stays, and releases remain historical.

Build 49 tracks oversight offices, inspector independence and credibility, procurement audits, conflict-of-interest disclosures, bid protests, protest decisions, compliance findings, corrective-action orders, contract stays, award rescissions, oversight-driven debarments, vendor self-attestations, independent verification, auditor capture and recusals, oversight incidents, and oversight crises. Crises can create places such as `contract stayed binary still running`, `successful protest against code already deployed`, `watchdog dependent on the system it watches`, `vendor auditing its own corrective action`, and `watchdog became another source of permission`, leaving the bailiff asking whether the auditor or the contract decides what is compliant.
